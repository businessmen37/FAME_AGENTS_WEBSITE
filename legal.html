01

Contact and responsibility

Fame Agent is the service brand behind fameagents.de, helping people and businesses turn ideas into practical projects. We work with clients in the United States, Europe and internationally. Contact us using the email or WhatsApp details listed on this page for project enquiries, questions about this website or privacy requests.

02

Working with Fame Agents

This website describes our services and invites project enquiries. Sending an enquiry does not create a contract or commit you to a purchase. Before work begins, we agree with you in writing on the scope, deliverables, fees, payment schedule, timing, revisions and the ownership or licensing of the final work. Cancellation and other project conditions are set out in the applicable agreement. We do not guarantee sales, audience growth, revenue or other commercial results.

03

Website content and intellectual property

Website text, branding, artwork and visual assets may be protected by intellectual property rights. Contact us before reusing material beyond uses permitted by law. Client assets and third-party material remain subject to their respective owners’ rights. AI-created team scenes and reference-based portraits are labelled on the website. Concept studies illustrate creative directions; they do not represent commissioned client projects or verified commercial results.

04

Questions and disputes

If you have a concern, email info@fameagents.de with the relevant project details so we can review it with you. Mandatory consumer rights and any rights available under applicable law remain unaffected by this website or a project agreement.

05

Information you choose to share

When you contact us, you may provide your name, email address, company or project name, selected services, message, budget and preferred timing. Project communications may also include materials you choose to share. Provide only information relevant to your request. Do not send passwords, payment-card details or sensitive personal information through the enquiry tools. Enquiry fields are used to prepare and respond to your request, not to create an account.

06

How the enquiry form works

The enquiry form prepares a project brief locally in your browser. It does not automatically send the brief to a website server. You choose whether to send it through your email application or WhatsApp. Selecting the WhatsApp brief link includes the brief in the link sent to WhatsApp. Downloading a brief saves a text file on your own device. The website code does not store the form content in cookies or local storage.

07

Why information is processed

We use enquiry information to respond to you, discuss your project and take steps you request before a possible contract. Where the GDPR applies, this processing is based on Article 6(1)(b). Necessary business correspondence and protecting the website or service may be based on legitimate interests under Article 6(1)(f), including responding to general questions and preventing misuse. Applicable legal recordkeeping is based on Article 6(1)(c). If a separate optional activity requires consent, we request it for that activity; you may withdraw consent without affecting processing that was lawful before withdrawal.

08

Website delivery, cookies and analytics

The website uses no advertising pixels, analytics scripts, embedded social feeds or remotely hosted fonts. Its code does not set cookies or use local storage. The hosting service receives technical connection information required to deliver and secure the site, which can include your IP address, request time, requested URL and browser information. This technical processing supports reliable delivery and protection of the website. Service providers apply their own logging and retention practices.

09

Email, WhatsApp and social media

Email, WhatsApp and social-media destinations are services outside this website. When you send an email, open WhatsApp or follow a social link, the relevant provider processes information under its own privacy policy. Providers may process information in other countries, where data-protection rules can differ. Choose the contact channel you prefer and review its privacy information before sharing personal data. Social links do not embed platform tracking scripts in this website.

10

Recipients and project collaborators

Hosting and communication providers process information as needed to deliver their services. Project information may be shared with collaborators or specialists where necessary for the work you request. We limit project sharing to relevant information and use appropriate confidentiality and data-protection arrangements. We do not use enquiry details for unrelated marketing without an appropriate legal basis.

11

Retention and security

We retain enquiries and project correspondence for as long as needed to handle your request, provide agreed services, meet applicable recordkeeping duties or establish, exercise or defend legal claims. Retention depends on the purpose and legal obligations applying to the record. We remove information when it is no longer needed for those purposes. Copies you download remain under your control, and external platforms apply their own retention rules. Access to project information is limited to the people and services that need it. No communication method can guarantee absolute security.

12

Your privacy rights

Where applicable, you can request access, correction, deletion, restriction or portability of your personal data. You can object to processing based on legitimate interests and withdraw consent for consent-based processing. These rights may be subject to legal conditions. Email info@fameagents.de with your request; proportionate identity verification may be needed. Where the GDPR applies, we normally respond within one month, subject to permitted extensions. You may also complain to a competent data-protection authority, including in your place of habitual residence, work or the alleged infringement.

13

Policy updates

This page describes the current website and enquiry tools as of 7 October 2026. We update it when our services, website features or processing practices change. The updated date helps you identify the latest version.
