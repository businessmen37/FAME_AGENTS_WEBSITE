How the enquiry form works

The form creates a project brief locally in your browser. It does not submit the form to a website server. You choose whether to send the brief through your email application or WhatsApp. Selecting the WhatsApp brief link places the brief into a WhatsApp URL and opens the third-party service. Downloading a brief saves a text file on your own device. The website code does not store form content in cookies or local storage.
